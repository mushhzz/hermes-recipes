## Interpretation

- Internal deployments, vendor methodology, randomized workplace experiments and survey associations support different conclusions.
- Generated code, merged PRs, perceived usefulness, estimated labor savings and production outcomes are not interchangeable measures.
- AWS guidance does not establish a deployed autonomous lifecycle. Amazon modernization and the attributed Mantle anecdote are separately labeled in the research.
- Versions of the Microsoft-linked pooled study are not independent replications. DORA summaries do not replace the report's statistical methods.
- Research recommendations are preserved as inferences, not silently adopted as current behavior.
- This archive retains distilled research and short excerpts, not full mirrors of source websites.
